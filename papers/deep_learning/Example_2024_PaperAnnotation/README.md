## 📝 Summary

### Problem Statement

Many researchers struggle to maintain organized notes and annotations on the papers they read, leading to lost insights and difficulty connecting ideas across papers.

### Proposed Solution

A structured template-based approach to paper annotation that ensures consistency and completeness while allowing for personal insights and connections.

### Methodology

The approach uses a standardized markdown template with sections for:
- Paper metadata and references
- Summary of key contributions
- Personal annotations and insights
- Experimental results
- Implementation notes
- Related work connections

## 🔑 Key Takeaways

- **Structured annotations** help retain information long-term
- **Consistent formatting** makes it easier to search and compare papers
- **Personal insights** are just as important as paper summaries
